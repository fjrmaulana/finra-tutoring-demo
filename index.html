Three ways to work together

Take one, or combine them as your exam date gets closer.

  • One-to-one sessions

    Sixty minutes online, on the topics you choose. We work real exam-style questions together and stop on every one you get wrong until the reasoning is clear.

    Best when one or two topics are holding you back

  • A study plan to your exam date

    A week-by-week schedule that fits around your job, with reading, question sets and a short check-in each week so nothing slides.

    Best when you are starting from scratch

  • Mock exam review

    Sit a full-length timed mock, then go through it with your tutor question by question. You leave with a short list of what to fix before test day.

    Best in the last two weeks

From first call to exam day

  1. Free call

    Twenty minutes to hear which exam you are taking, when, and how study has gone so far.

  2. Diagnostic quiz

    A short question set across every exam section shows where your marks are being lost.

  3. Weekly sessions

    We work through your weak sections first, with question sets to finish between sessions.

  4. Mock exam and review

    A timed full-length mock about ten days out, reviewed together, so exam day holds no surprises.
